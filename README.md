# KasiQuote

## The problem

Gardeners, cleaners, home helpers, and other part-time or independent workers in South Africa often agree on prices in person or over WhatsApp. It’s easy to forget expenses like materials and taxi fare when deciding what to charge. Writing a clear quote can also take time, especially when English is an additional language. Once the work is done, it can be hard to keep track of what customers paid, what each job cost, and how much money is left at the end of the month.

KasiQuote helps workers estimate job costs, work out a price, and create a plain-English quote they can copy into WhatsApp. They can record payments against saved quotes or add income from jobs they agreed on separately. The earnings view brings payments and job expenses together so they can see what they took home and plan their spending.

## How to run it

1. Open KasiQuote on your phone. [Add the tested app link or download instructions here.]
2. In **Quote**, enter the job name, estimated hours, hourly rate, materials, and transport. Tap **Generate quote** to see the suggested price and save the job.
3. Tap **Copy WhatsApp message**, paste the quote into your customer’s chat, and send it.
4. In **Jobs**, mark the quote accepted and the job completed as you go. When the customer pays, tap **Record payment** and enter the amount you received.
5. For a job you didn’t quote in the app, go to **Earnings → Add income**. Enter the amount received, the payment date, and any job costs you want to include.
6. In **Earnings**, choose **This month**, **Last month**, or **Last week**. The graph updates automatically with payments from both sources. The summary shows total payments, recorded job costs, and take-home earnings.

## How we kept Copilot token usage low

- We gave Copilot one focused brief covering quotes, payments, and earnings.
- We used plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to keep the build small.
- We wrote the README ourselves and tested the main workflow by hand.
